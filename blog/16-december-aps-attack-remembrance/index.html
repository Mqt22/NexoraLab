PAKISTAN · REMEMBRANCE · 16 DECEMBER

16 December: Remembering the Army Public School Peshawar Tragedy

Some dates remain part of a nation's memory. On 16 December, Pakistan remembers the children, teachers and staff whose lives were taken in the attack on Army Public School, Peshawar, in 2014.

Every year, 16 December brings back the memory of a tragedy that changed countless families and left a lasting mark on Pakistan. It is a day to pause, remember the innocent lives lost and recognise the grief that families and survivors continue to carry.

What happened on 16 December 2014?

On the morning of 16 December 2014, terrorists attacked the Army Public School in Peshawar, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Students and school staff were inside the campus when the attack began. The assault took the lives of 147 people, including 132 schoolchildren and 15 members of staff, according to reporting by Dawn on the tenth anniversary.

The United Nations Children's Fund (UNICEF) condemned the attack on the day it happened, describing the killing of children at school as an appalling loss and reminding the world that every child has the right to learn in safety. Read UNICEF's statement from 16 December 2014.

Remembering the lives, not only the date

Behind every number was a child with a name, a family, friendships and plans for the future. There were parents expecting their children to return home, classmates who lost friends and teachers whose work was rooted in helping young people learn.

Remembrance should never reduce these lives to a statistic. It should make space for the families who continue to grieve, the survivors whose experiences remain with them and everyone who still feels the weight of that day.

Why the right to education must be protected

A school should be a place where children can read, ask questions, make friends and imagine the future. No student should have to fear violence simply for attending a lesson. The APS tragedy remains a painful reminder that protecting schools and the people inside them must be a shared responsibility.

Remembering 16 December also means supporting a culture in which children can pursue education safely, teachers can do their work without fear and violence against students is rejected without qualification.

A moment of reflection on 16 December

As the anniversary approaches, let the day be observed with dignity and care. Remember the children and staff, stand with the families and survivors, and keep the value of every child's life and education at the centre of the conversation.

We remember.

To the students, teachers and staff of Army Public School, Peshawar: your memory remains with the nation. May Allah grant the departed the highest place in Jannah and give patience and strength to their families.

This article is published as a remembrance, not as a political statement or a source of graphic detail. The focus is on the people whose lives were lost and the importance of protecting children and education.
